# 12-Month Editorial Calendar

**Publication rule that governs everything below:** treatment-specific content is
published only after (a) clinical leadership confirms the practice can
appropriately evaluate or provide the service, and (b) a named provider has
reviewed the article. Every clinical post carries the reviewing provider's name,
credentials, review date, and a link to their biography.

Nothing in Phase 2 or later may publish while the corresponding service is still
tier `planned`.

## Per-article requirements

Every article, before publication:

- [ ] Search intent identified and matched by the format
- [ ] One primary keyword, not competing with an existing page
- [ ] Title under ~60 characters where practical, keyword front-loaded
- [ ] Meta description 150 to 160 characters
- [ ] One H1, logical H2/H3 beneath it
- [ ] Minimum two internal links, at least one to a service or conversion page
- [ ] One original image with descriptive alt text (no stock)
- [ ] Named provider reviewer with credentials and date, for any clinical content
- [ ] Medical disclaimer block
- [ ] One clear CTA, matched to the reader's stage
- [ ] Voice check: no em dashes, Oxford commas, no nautical puns, no guarantees, no unsubstantiated superlatives
- [ ] `BlogPosting` schema populated with real dates

## What not to write

- Anything implying dentistry treats unrelated medical conditions
- Anything promising a pain-free experience or a guaranteed result
- Comparative content naming another local practice (Brand Guide §7: a legal and reputational risk in a small market)
- Thin "5 tips" listicles with no local relevance
- Content about a service the practice does not provide on site
